CONFIDENTIAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE OF HIRING PROJECT BASED CONTRACTUAL CIVILIANS INTRODUCTION 1. Naval Research and Development Institute (NRDI) is envisaged to be the hub of Pakistan Navy (PN) - Research and Development (R&D) activities through active collaboration with academia and local industry. End objective is to execute projects to deliver products to PN with total control on design and fully commensurate with contemporary technologies. R&D work requires hiring of Project based Human Resource (HR) having 2. specialized technical skills. Permanent HRI Naval Personnel are not sufficient to undertake diversified projects. Hence, the gap is filled with hiring of specialized Project based HR directly from the local market on contract. 3. NRDI is the engine and nerve center of R&D activities in PN. In such knowledge and technology-intensive organizations, works is the primary capability enabler, and outcomes are directly dependent on the quality, continuity and motivation of personnel. An effective induction and retention policy is critical to ensure that personnel are integrated quickly, perform effectively, and remain committed long enough to deliver operational value. Weak induction and poor retention directly translate into capability gaps, increased risk, and loss of institutional knowledge. AIM 4. The aim of this policy is to enable induction, competency development and retention of top talent of contract employees of R&D wing. GENERAL POLICIES 5. The policy is applicable to all contract employees and the staff responsible for handling their matters at R&D wing and HQ NRDI. This document is initiated and circulated by Human Resource Department and is to be amended as needed. A copy of this document must be present with all respective departments, and electronic copy is available on rdw information system.
